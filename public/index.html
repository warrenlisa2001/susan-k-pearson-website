About Susan

The Work Begins
With Attention

I bring presence, experience, curiosity, and careful attention to what is seeking to be understood. I do not believe healing is something that is done to a person. Each individual carries an innate capacity for balance, insight, and transformation — and my role is to create the conditions in which that process can unfold.


I will meet you where you are. I listen deeply, observe carefully, and support your process — drawing on decades of experience and a range of modalities as tools, not as the focus. The relationship comes first. The person comes first. The rest unfolds from there.

Beyond the Studio

For many years, I have quietly dedicated my personal time, resources, and energy to the care of vulnerable animals.

What began as individual acts of compassion gradually became something much greater than I originally imagined. I came to realise that I am not simply caring for animals — I am building something. Something that reflects my values. Something grounded in compassion, responsibility, stewardship, and respect for all living creatures. Most importantly, something that can endure.

The Animal Advocate Initiative exists to honour life and respect all creatures — to create the conditions in which life, in all its forms, can flourish with dignity.
