Cat Rescue

Alongside my healing practice, I am deeply committed to the welfare of cats in Dubai and beyond. This page is dedicated to that work.

The Mission

Every animal deserves safety and love

Dubai's streets are home to many cats in need of care, medical attention, and rehoming. I work with local volunteers and rescue networks to trap, neuter, and rehome cats whenever possible.

Meet Jessie — Susan's Westie and the rescue operation's most dedicated (non-feline) volunteer. Jessie has appointed herself official caretaker to every cat that crosses the threshold. New arrivals hiss. Jessie is unmoved. Eventually, they cuddle. It works every time.

How You Can Help

Adopt

Give a rescued cat a forever home. Reach out to learn about cats currently available for adoption.

Foster

Provide temporary care while a cat awaits adoption. Even a few weeks makes an enormous difference.

Donate

Support vet bills, neutering programmes, and food supplies for cats in our care.
