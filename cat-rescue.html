Dubai Cat Rescue

Susan's commitment to healing extends to the most vulnerable creatures in our community. A portion of every session goes directly to supporting abandoned and street cats across the UAE.

The Mission

Every healing session contributes

Dubai and the UAE are home to thousands of abandoned and street cats — some surrendered by expat families, others born on the streets. These animals often face harsh conditions, illness, and injury without anyone to advocate for them.

Susan has been involved in street cat rescue for years, working with local volunteers and vetted rescue organisations to fund veterinary care, spay and neuter programmes, foster placements, and international rehoming for cats that cannot find homes in the UAE.

A portion of proceeds from every healing session Susan conducts goes directly to this work. When you invest in your own healing, you also contribute to the wellbeing of some of the most vulnerable animals in our community.
