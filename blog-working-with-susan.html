The Healing Journey · The Work

What Actually Happens When You Work With Susan

An honest, intimate account — from first contact to the weeks that follow.

The session begins before you even lie down.

It begins in the first message you send — the one where you finally decide to reach out. Something has shifted. Something is ready. Maybe you've tried other things. Maybe this is completely new territory. Either way, there is a quiet knowing that brought you here, and Susan honours that from the very first exchange.

Before You Arrive

The intake form is not a formality. Susan reads every word carefully before she meets you. She wants to understand your history, your patterns, the things you've been carrying. By the time you walk through the door, she already has a sense of you — not a diagnosis, not a plan, but a felt sense of what your system might need.

Many clients describe feeling strangely calm as they arrive. The studio itself does something. The dark walls, the carefully chosen art, the scent in the air — it signals to your nervous system: you can put it down now.

"I walked in carrying the weight of months. Within minutes I felt something begin to soften that I hadn't even known was held." — Client, Dublin

The Conversation

Every session begins with a conversation — never rushed, never clinical. Susan asks questions, but more than that, she listens in a way that most people rarely experience. She listens to what is said and to what lives beneath it. She listens to the body language, the pauses, the quality of the breath. She is already reading the session before it has formally begun.

This conversation is where you are truly met. Clients often say they feel seen in a way they didn't anticipate — not exposed, but witnessed. There is a profound difference. Susan has a gift for creating safety without softening the truth of what she perceives.

On the Table

When you lie down, something remarkable begins. Susan works across multiple levels simultaneously — physical, energetic, and subconscious — with a seamlessness that is difficult to describe and impossible to manufacture. One moment her hands are moving across the energy field above the body. The next, they are applying precise pressure to a point of held tension in the abdomen. The next, her voice is guiding you gently into a subconscious process.

It does not feel like three different things happening. It feels like one continuous movement of intelligence — like your system is being read and responded to in real time.

Many clients fall into a state that is neither sleep nor waking — a deeply receptive place where the nervous system finally stops its vigilance. It is in this state that the most profound shifts often occur. Patterns that have been held for years begin to loosen. Old stories begin to lose their charge. The body remembers how to rest.

"I fell asleep. I never fall asleep. I woke up feeling like something had been reorganised inside me." — S.B., Dubai

After the Session

Susan does not rush you off the table. She gives you time to return — to feel the ground beneath you, to notice what has shifted. There is a brief conversation after, gentle and orienting. She will often share something she noticed, an image that arose, a feeling in a particular area of the body. These observations tend to land with uncanny precision.

Every session includes post-session integration guidance. This might be a suggestion for how to spend the rest of the day, a practice to support what has been opened, or simply permission to rest. Susan understands that the work continues after you leave the room — the days that follow a session are often the most potent.

What People Notice in the Days That Follow

Clients report different things. Some notice clarity — a kind of mental spaciousness that hadn't been there before. Some notice that a situation which previously caused anxiety now feels navigable. Some notice they are sleeping differently. Some notice that a relationship dynamic has shifted — not because anything external changed, but because something in them has.

Some clients notice very little at first — and then, three days later, something opens. This is common. The body integrates at its own pace. Susan will tell you: the work does not end when you leave. It is still moving.

Why People Come Back

Most clients return. Not because they are dependent — Susan actively encourages autonomy and integration — but because the work reveals new layers. Each session opens a new depth. There is always more to discover, not because something is wrong, but because growth is infinite and the body is endlessly intelligent.

The question is not whether you are ready. The question is whether you are willing to be met — fully, precisely, and with extraordinary care.
